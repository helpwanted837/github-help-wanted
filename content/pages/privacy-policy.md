GitHub Help Wanted is a static content site. We aim to minimize data collection.

## Key Takeaways

- No account required; we don’t sell personal information.
- Infrastructure providers may process basic request data (delivery and security).
- Analytics, if enabled, is aggregated and privacy-friendly.
- Cookies are limited and may be used by security/analytics tooling.
- Email is voluntary; avoid sending sensitive data.

## Data We May Process

- Request metadata (IP address, user agent, URL, timestamp, status code)
- Aggregated analytics (page views and high-level usage patterns)
- Voluntary emails you send us

## Third Parties

We may use providers such as Cloudflare (CDN/security) and GitHub (source hosting). Their systems may process request data as part of service delivery.

## Your Choices

- Block cookies in your browser.
- Use a privacy-focused browser or content blocker.
- Contact us with questions.
