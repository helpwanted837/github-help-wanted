We publish developer guides that are meant to be used, not just read.

## Key Takeaways

- Reader-first: clear intent, steps, and outcomes.
- Verifiable: references prioritize official docs, standards, and reputable research.
- Safe defaults: least privilege, reversible changes, and validation steps.
- Transparent: limitations and affiliations are disclosed.

## Standards

- Original writing (no long-copying from sources).
- Clear structure (headings, tables where helpful, short FAQs when needed).
- Explicit assumptions (hosting, repo layout, versions when relevant).

## Corrections & Updates

Email us with the page URL, the section heading, and a primary source that supports the correction.

## Disclosures

If a page contains affiliate links or sponsored access, the disclosure is placed close to the link.
