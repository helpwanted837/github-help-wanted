## Key Takeaways

- Include the page URL and what you expected to happen.
- Add reproduction steps (screenshots/logs if relevant).
- Don’t send secrets (API keys, passwords, private tokens).

## What To Send

- Content corrections (broken steps, outdated UI labels, wrong claims)
- Requests for new topics
- Partnership inquiries (with clear constraints)
- Privacy requests related to communications you initiated

## Response Time

We try to reply within **3–5 business days**. Complex requests may take longer.
